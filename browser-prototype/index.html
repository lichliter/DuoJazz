About this experiment

Browser synth only. No microphone, accounts, or pitch grading. This does not verify the native iPad app, Safari, or device audio. Space plays or pauses. Left/Right move steps. R restarts and silences leftover audio.

Excerpt: Autumn Leaves opening eight bars in concert G minor, after the jbpicon Ultimate Guitar chart (key Gm, no capo, Verse 1, last update Aug 21 2025): Cm7 F7 B♭maj7 E♭maj7 Am7♭5 D7 Gm6 Gm6. Tonic bars use Gm6 as on that chart. This is a contiguous excerpt for placing a lick, not the tune’s head.

Phrase: Cry Me a River minor-tonic cell 9–1–5–♭3–2–1, one bar, eighth–eighth–quarter–eighth–eighth–quarter. Concert G minor: A4 G4 D4 Bb3 A3 G3. Not DuoJazz iivi-cry-me-enclosure, a major ii–V–I double enclosure.

Two applications of that same cell: Minor home (G-minor shape on Gm, bar 7) and Altered dominant (E♭-minor shape over D7, bar 6, then a composed resolution into Gm). Isolated hear/echo of the naked cell is optional and is not required before the eight-bar demonstration.
