# Workbook application mappings

This audit covers both pages for each of the thirty licks in the [bass-clef workbook](essential-jazz-vocabulary-bass-clef.pdf): printed pages 1–60. PDF page numbers are four higher.

The catalog contains 117 application contexts and 204 selectable versions. Applications describe a harmonic use; versions capture starting degrees, quality changes, partial cells, or demonstrated timing. The continuation pages are part of the inventory, not merely the introductory diagrams.

These are interval-based, workbook-guided practice versions. They retain the vocabulary cells and their harmonic relationships; they do not reproduce every composed connecting phrase, rhythmic ornament, or arbitrary permutation on the example pages. Where a passage combines cells, the app uses the chosen cell alone.

Each version has a playable workbook application example. Chart suggestions require the stated root movement, chord families, and enough time for every sounding note. Matches are harmonic applications of vocabulary, not transcriptions of tune melodies or claims that the workbook names those tunes. Optional color tones extend the written harmony.

Long patterns are not silently played across an incompatible change. A shorter body or one-bar cell is a separately selectable version. If no chart supports the chosen version, **Practice workbook example** remains available; these generated backing exercises are labeled separately and are not added to My charts.

## Source interpretation

- Bread and Butter (pp. 51–52): the minor-seventh use retains the original shape rooted a whole step below the chord. The dominant use changes the second half to a third-inversion half-diminished arpeggio; it is not a transposition of the major form.
- Continuation-page examples introduce additional contexts, including EMR resolving to major (p. 4), Donna Lee Resolution into minor (p. 16), dominant-II arpeggiated voice-leading (p. 18), upper-structure arpeggios (p. 22), and minor-key CBC (p. 26). Their examples and versions cite the continuation page.
- Lydian Grip (p. 53): the “13th” prose conflicts with the engraved F grip over G13. The implementation follows the engraved flat-seventh grip. The final paragraph refers to a dominant flat fifth while its engraving shows an A-flat grip over D half-diminished, continuing over G7 flat nine. Those two chord contexts are mapped separately. Page 54 supplies the additional grip placements.
- Bird’s Chromatic Frenzy: the unusual half-diminished / half-diminished / minor setting on p. 55 is preserved as engraved. Its ordinary half-diminished / dominant / minor continuation on p. 56 is a separate context.
- Decorated Chord Tones explicitly generalizes diatonic decoration to other qualities. Versions cover major, minor, dominant, half-diminished, and diminished, plus the dominant color forms and cadence contexts. The two-bar body is separate from the full pattern with its sustained final note.
- Sandwich Enclosure generalizes to diatonic targets. Root, third, and fifth presets follow each quality’s scale, rather than uniformly shifting a dominant-third enclosure onto every target.
- The entries describe harmonic use rather than insisting every lick has multiple applications. Some, such as Sonny Stitt Resolution and Triad to Pentatonic Resolution, remain focused on the contexts actually shown.

## Implementation and checks

- `js/workbook-applications.js` holds the musical definitions, source pages, exact interval variants, and harmony patterns.
- `js/practice-catalog.js` matches complete sounding windows and generates application/variant lessons plus reference exercise forms. Exercise harmony uses the relevant dominant alterations.
- Explicit spot elements and beat offsets are shared by notation and playback. Pickup notes keep their negative onsets. Existing Learn/Master content, settings keys, and grading behavior stay intact.
- `js/workbook-applications.test.mjs` checks all thirty inventories, independent note fixtures, continuation contexts, negative harmony cases, every example version, notation/audio agreement, and half-bar/pickup timing.
- The engraving suite round-trips every lesson through all keys, instruments, and notation modes. Browser review checks every module’s application inventory and representative chart, version, and no-match flows.
